About Our Project

Bridging linguistic barriers through innovative AI-powered transliteration technology

Project Overview

The Transliterations Tool for Street Signs is a comprehensive web application developed by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) to address the critical challenge of linguistic accessibility across India's diverse linguistic landscape.

This innovative tool leverages cutting-edge AI technology from AI4Bharat's IndicXlit to provide accurate transliteration between 10 major Indian language scripts, with a special focus on improving street sign readability and promoting cultural communication.

Our Mission

To democratize language accessibility across India by providing free, accurate, and user-friendly transliteration tools that preserve phonetic integrity while bridging script barriers.

  • Enable seamless communication across linguistic communities
  • Preserve cultural heritage through accurate phonetic transliteration
  • Support Digital India initiatives through technological innovation
  • Promote inclusive education and accessibility

Our Vision

To create a linguistically unified India where language scripts are no longer barriers to communication, education, and cultural exchange.

  • Universal accessibility to multilingual content
  • Enhanced cross-cultural understanding
  • Technological empowerment of linguistic minorities
  • Seamless integration of traditional and modern communication

Technology & Innovation

AI4Bharat IndicXlit

State-of-the-art neural transliteration engine specifically designed for Indian languages, achieving >90% accuracy in phonetic preservation.

Tesseract.js OCR

Advanced Optical Character Recognition for extracting text from street signs and images, supporting multiple Indian scripts.

Progressive Web App

Client-side JavaScript implementation ensuring fast performance, offline capability, and cross-platform compatibility.

Accessibility-First Design

WCAG 2.1 compliant interface with screen reader support, high contrast modes, and comprehensive keyboard navigation.

Supported Language Scripts

Our tool supports transliteration between English and 9 major Indian language scripts, totaling 10 languages:
