Training Tips & Examples

Phonetic Training

For sound-based transliteration:

  • hello → ஹலோ
  • computer → கம்ப்யூட்டர்
  • internet → இண்டர்நெட்

Semantic Training

For meaning-based transliteration:

  • good → நல்ல
  • water → தண்ணீர்
  • home → வீடு

Training Requirements Examples

You can train the AI with different requirements:

  • Phonetic emphasis: Focus on sound-based transliteration
  • Semantic emphasis: Focus on meaning-based transliteration
  • Mixed mode: Combination of both approaches
  • Custom patterns: Specific word pairs you want to learn
  • Rule-based: Grammatical rules for word formation
